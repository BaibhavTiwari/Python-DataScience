## Core statistics on returns (Day 14)

- **Sample mean**: The average of observed values in a sample, used as an estimate of the true (population) mean; for returns, it is the average return over a given period.

- **Sample variance**: A measure of how spread out sample values are around the sample mean, computed using squared deviations and usually dividing by (n − 1) to make it an unbiased estimator.

- **Sample standard deviation**: The square root of the sample variance, expressing dispersion in the same units as the original data (e.g., daily return), commonly used as a volatility measure.

- **Sampling distribution of the mean**: The distribution of sample means obtained by repeatedly taking samples from the same population; for returns, it describes how average return estimates vary across different samples.

- **Standard error of the mean**: The standard deviation of the sampling distribution of the mean, quantifying how much the sample mean is expected to fluctuate around the true mean; it decreases as sample size increases.
